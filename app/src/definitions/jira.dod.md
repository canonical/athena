# Jira Definition Of Done

A Jira ticket is good enough when:

1. The title is clear and specific.
2. The description explains the expected outcome and relevant context.
3. Acceptance criteria are defined.
4. The ticket is placed at the correct Jira level: Objective, Epic, Story, Task, or Subtask.
5. The parent-child relationship is defined correctly.
6. Dependencies are captured when they exist.
7. Priority is defined.
8. The owner is clear.
9. The content has been reviewed and approved by the required persona and the user where applicable.
10. All discussions and approvals defined by the responsibility rules have been completed.
11. The scope is small enough for its Jira level.
12. If the ticket is a lowest-level item, it has an hour-based estimate and clear execution scope.
